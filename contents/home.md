I am an MPhil student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), supervised by Prof. [Jiayi Huang](https://jyhuang91.github.io/) and Prof. [Zhijiang Guo](https://cartus.github.io/). My research focuses on systems for large language models, especially reinforcement learning infrastructure, distributed training, and efficient inference. I am interested in AI algorithm and system co-design to make training and inference more efficient.

My current work explores KV cache reuse for asynchronous LLM reinforcement learning, dynamic GPU allocation between training and rollout workers, and inference optimization on Ascend NPUs. I also contribute to the Huawei Ascend community through operator development and programming competitions. Previously, I worked on GPU power management at Harbin Institute of Technology and MLIR-based processing-in-memory (PIM) compilers at Seoul National University.
