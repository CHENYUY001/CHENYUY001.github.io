# University emblem sources

These emblems identify the institutions in the site's education history.

- `hkust-gz.png`: https://hexagonstar.github.io/images/hkustgz.png (the education layout reference supplied by the site owner).
- `snu.png`: https://en.snu.ac.kr/webdata/uploads/eng/image/2020/02/about-symbol-bi_lg.png, from https://en.snu.ac.kr/about/symbol/ui. Cropped to the blue emblem on the left of the source image.
- `hit.png`: https://www.hit.edu.cn/_upload/article/images/d3/ec/8fcaa5d24cb59a8e9660324ef50b/735df70a-538b-4bd6-8e52-3f373085a616.png, from https://www.hit.edu.cn/12254/list.htm.

The emblems belong to their respective universities and are not covered by the site's code license.

All three emblems have been trimmed to their visible bounds and proportionally centered on matching transparent square canvases for alignment.
